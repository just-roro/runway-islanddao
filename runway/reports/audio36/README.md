## Mixing and lifecycle

Single AudioContext, cached decode with four concurrent requests, UI/voice-first loading, 10-second request timeouts and retry on the next gesture. Missing/unready one-shots use a short original synthesized fallback; loading completion never replays an old event. SFX compressor, priority-based eviction, max 10 one-shot voices plus at most three crossfading ambient sources. AudioContext runs only after a valid gesture. SFX mute/background stops voices; pause stops game voices/ambience and preserves music position; trusted gestures recover suspended context. No simulation, server, rules, collision, route, shop price or music asset changed.
